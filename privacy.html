Vantoryx Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Vantoryx (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides an informational sports-intelligence app that shows live sportsbook odds and AI-assisted game analysis. Vantoryx does not place bets, accept wagers, or operate a sportsbook.

Information we process

Information you provide in the app
Questions you submit to Ask A.I. These are sent to a Supabase Edge Function so the app can return an answer. We process the question text only as needed to provide that answer.

Information stored on your device
Your recently viewed game analyses (up to 50 entries), stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. You can clear this history in the app (History > Clear, and from Profile > Your data when that screen ships). App preferences, such as favorite leagues and display settings, are stored locally on your device.

Information processed through third-party services
Live odds data is provided through The Odds API, via a Supabase Edge Function. The Odds API key is held server-side only and is not embedded in the app. Supabase provides backend and Edge Function infrastructure for the odds and Ask A.I. features. Apple handles subscriptions and payments. We do not receive or store your full payment card details; Apple processes payment under its own terms. Advertising (free tier only) is provided by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob — see “Advertising” below.

Accounts
Vantoryx v1 is designed for guest use and does not currently require a sign-in account. If sign-in is added later, this policy will be updated to describe the account data collected, how to delete the account, and what happens to stored data on deletion.

How we use information

  • To show live odds, game details, and market analysis.
  • To answer Ask A.I. questions.
  • To remember your history and preferences on your device.
  • To determine whether you have an active Vantoryx Pro subscription, based on Apple’s purchase status, so Pro features (including Unlimited Ask A.I. and ad-free use) can be unlocked.
  • To show ads to free users, and to hide ads from Pro users.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

We do not sell personal information.

Subscriptions

Vantoryx Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription:

  • Monthly: $4.99 per month
  • Yearly: $49.99 per year

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, through your Apple ID subscription settings. Cancellation and refund requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms.

Free use includes 5 Ask A.I. questions per day, with ads. Vantoryx Pro includes Unlimited Ask A.I. and removes ads.

Advertising

Free users see banner ads at the bottom of the Home screen. Pro users do not see ads.

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob may collect information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data, under Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Where required, the app will ask for your permission to track you across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on tracking. You can also change tracking permission later in your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Ads are provided by third parties and do not imply that Vantoryx endorses the advertised product or service. Vantoryx does not place bets and does not operate a sportsbook, regardless of any ad shown.

Data retention and deletion

  • Local history and preferences remain on your device until you clear them in the app or delete the app.
  • Ask A.I. questions are processed to return an answer; do not submit sensitive personal information in Ask A.I. questions.
  • If you have questions about data, or want to request deletion of any server-side data associated with your use, contact us at boggs.charlesr@gmail.com.

Children’s privacy

Vantoryx is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted at this URL, with a new effective date.
